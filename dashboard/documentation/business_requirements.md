# Business Requirements

## Objective

Develop a reliable fundraising-performance view that helps nonprofit leadership understand donor behavior, campaign results, acquisition effectiveness, volunteer engagement, and solicitation response across FY21-FY26.

## Stakeholders

- Executive and development leadership
- Fundraising and donor-stewardship teams
- Campaign managers
- Data and CRM administrators

## Business questions

1. How have active donors, donor retention, and giving changed by fiscal year?
2. What share of donors are new, retained, or reactivated?
3. Which acquisition sources produce the strongest donor conversion?
4. Do donor-volunteers demonstrate stronger repeat giving?
5. Which campaigns meet or exceed their goals?
6. Which solicitation channels produce the strongest response?
7. Are source-system limitations distorting year-over-year comparisons?

## Acceptance criteria

- Preserve all valid source records and signed financial transactions.
- Standardize identifiers, dates, categories, names, and contact fields.
- Maintain auditable raw-to-clean transformations.
- Validate primary-key uniqueness and cross-table relationships.
- Use a September-August fiscal year consistently.
- Distinguish full-data reporting from comparable stable-source reporting.
- Publish executive dashboards with documented definitions and limitations.
